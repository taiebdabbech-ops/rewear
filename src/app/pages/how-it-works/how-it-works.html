How REWEAR works

REWEAR connects people who no longer wear their clothes with people looking for quality pieces at fair prices.

Buying guide

  1. Browse a category or search for a brand, then filter by size, price and condition.
  2. Check the condition grade, the photos and the seller's badge and reviews.
  3. Contact the seller and agree on delivery or a meeting place.
  4. Receive the item, check it, pay, and leave a review.
Start shopping

Selling guide

  1. Create your free account.
  2. Click Sell, add up to four photos in daylight.
  3. Fill in the size, brand, condition and price; be honest about defects.
  4. Answer buyers quickly and mark the item as sold in My account.

About

REWEAR is a student web project from SUP'COM. Its goal is to cut clothing costs, give clothes a second life and help people earn money from what they no longer wear.

Terms and privacy

Demonstration project: data is stored locally by json-server and is not shared. Do not use real passwords.
